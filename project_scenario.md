A Day in the Life of Marcus
Morning
Marcus opens the equipment room for a small community sports center. A coach asks to borrow ten basketballs for practice, but Marcus is not sure how many are already checked out. “If I lend out more than we have, another team will be stuck,” he thinks.

Late Morning
A tennis instructor returns two rackets, but one has a cracked frame. Marcus writes it down on a sticky note, then realizes damaged equipment should not be available for checkout until it is repaired or replaced.

Lunch
During lunch, Marcus reviews the afternoon schedule. Two teams have requested the same set of training cones at overlapping times. “Reservations need times, not just item names,” he says.

Afternoon
A student comes in to return soccer balls late from yesterday’s practice. Marcus wonders whether late returns should be flagged, especially when another group was waiting for the same items.

Evening
Before closing, Marcus looks around the equipment room. Some items are checked out, some are damaged, and some were returned late. “We need a simple way to track availability, condition, reservations, and responsibility,” he thinks.
